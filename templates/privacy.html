Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Spendly is built around a simple principle: your financial data is yours. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do — and don't do — with it.

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly's expense tracking service, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — the email address and password you provide when registering. We use your email to identify your account and, if you opt in, to send you service-related notifications.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any other details you enter about your transactions.
  • Usage data — basic, anonymised information about how you interact with Spendly (pages visited, features used) so we can improve the product.
  • Device information — your browser type and operating system, used to diagnose issues and optimise the experience.

We do not collect financial account numbers, card numbers, or bank credentials. Spendly is a manual tracker — you enter what you choose to enter, and nothing more.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Spendly's features.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account from unauthorised access.
  • Generate the summaries, reports, and category breakdowns you request.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or technical issues.

We do not use your expense data to train advertising models, build credit profiles, or share with data brokers. We will never sell your personal data — full stop.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database hosted with a reputable cloud provider. We apply industry-standard protections: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and restricted access controls for our internal team.

While no system is perfectly secure, we work continuously to protect your information. If we ever discover a breach that affects your data, we will notify you by email and post a notice on this page.

You may export or permanently delete your data at any time from your account settings. Deletion is irreversible and removes your account, expenses, and associated records.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third parties to operate. They only receive the minimum information needed to perform their role:

  • Hosting provider — stores the database and serves the application.
  • Email service — delivers transactional emails such as password resets and security alerts.
  • Analytics — measures aggregate usage to help us understand which features are most useful. We use anonymised metrics and do not track individual expense entries.

We vet every third party for security and privacy practices. We will update this list as our stack evolves.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests about this privacy policy or your data, please reach out at support@spendly.app. We typically respond within two business days.

For data access or deletion requests, please email us from the address associated with your account so we can verify your identity.
